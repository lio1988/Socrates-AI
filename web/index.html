    02 The method

    Why a council instead of one answer?

    Socrates treats an answer as something to examine, revise and reconstruct—not something to accept because it arrived fluently.

    1. 01

      Question

      Socrates isolates the assumption the answer depends on.

    2. 02

      Challenge

      Independent roles attack reasoning, evidence and hidden assumptions.

    3. 03

      Revision

      Agents are expected to update when criticism succeeds.

    4. 04

      Synthesis

      The surviving reasoning is reconstructed into a final answer.

    05

    Ratification

    The final synthesis is independently checked against the council's epistemic discipline before release.

    Evaluation remains separate from individual agent positions.

    03 Transparency

    Built in public reasoning.

    Inspect the process without pretending that private chain-of-thought is a product feature.
